## How Polycheck works

Rival frontier models answer a question blind, challenge each other, and a judge from a different model family rules. No model ever grades its own family.

| Fan-out | Cross-examine | Judge | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question routed to rival frontier models, blind | Models challenge each other's answers | Neutral judge from a different model family | One answer, plus the dissent and a full audit trail |
| parallel · retries · fallbacks · cost controls | structured rebuttal | never self-family | abstains rather than guesses |

Ranked first against tested frontier baselines over 1,000 blind prompts using LM Arena's published methodology — position-bias controlled, Bradley-Terry scored. Independent rerun in progress.
